# Investment memo / valuation / decision

- Investment question: how much, when, for which milestones, in which instrument, and for whom? Distinguish project payback vs investor recovery vs exit valuation.
- Month-by-month funded cash balance + minimum cash reserve drives tranche proposal (not EBITDA). Optimize timing only under constraints of commitments, buffers and probability of follow-on funding; an unfunded negative balance is a blocking risk.
- Equity: pre-money, investment, post-money=pre+investment, ownership=investment/post (subject to option pools, dilution, preferred terms). Avoid fabricated valuation. Show cap table before/after and assumptions about option pool/liquidation preference.
- Investor return: calculate investor-specific monthly investments (negative), dividends/exits/redemptions (positive), XIRR/IRR where defined, MOIC=total positive proceeds / total paid in. No promised exit, no meaningful IRR without cash distribution assumptions.
- Debt: interest, payment schedules, DSCR if there is operating debt capacity, covenants and maturity/refinancing risk. Grants separately if nonrepayable, with timing/conditions.
- NPV: discount **unlevered project cash flows** at project discount rate; investor NPV/IRR uses investor cash flows and their opportunity rate. If terminal value material, label it and sensitivity-test.
- Include at least three scenarios; assess downside survivability and assumptions that most change payback/funding/valuation.
- Verdict: GO (evidence and economics support next milestone), VALIDATE (key assumptions unconfirmed), PIVOT (current strategy poor but alternative plausible), NO-GO (fundamental constraints). Explain decision gates, not arbitrary 1–100 score.
