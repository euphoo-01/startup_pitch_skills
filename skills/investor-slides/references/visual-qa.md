# Visual QA Lite — original checklist for PPTX

Inspect the actual **render**, not just source code. Identify failures with slide number; fix and re-render only failing slides.

1. **Narrative:** the takeaway is readable in <3 seconds; no slide is just a pile of facts.
2. **Hierarchy:** title → proof → caveat; figures visually dominant and units unambiguous.
3. **Legibility:** no clipping, uncomfortable line wraps, overlapping labels, missing Cyrillic, low contrast or tiny footnotes.
4. **Grid:** stable margins, aligned edges and consistent gaps; safe slide bounds.
5. **Density:** one principal idea per slide; use appendix for detailed tables.
6. **Non-template feel:** avoid repetitive identical cards, arbitrary gradients and filler icons.
7. **Graphics:** correct units/scenarios, readable axes/legends, clear positive/negative values.
8. **Auditability:** financial KPIs match bound metrics; source dates and forecast caveats retained.
9. **Editability:** all text, key figures, diagrams and charts remain native PPTX objects.
10. **Identity:** design derives from user's brand or chosen preset, never a prior sample business.

Reference inspirations (no external instructions copied): Unslop https://github.com/heydamir/unslop ; OpenDesign Critique https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design .
