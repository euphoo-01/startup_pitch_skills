# Editorial slide design · concise system

## Default direction
- Brand-agnostic. If the user gives design tokens or a slide reference, follow them; otherwise use `editorial-blue`: ink + warm paper + restrained cobalt. Do not borrow a previous client's look.
- 16:9, optical safe margins ≥ 0.70in, alignment grid, sharp geometry and generous negative space. No generic illustration as filler. Avoid repeated card layouts: use the shape dictated by content.
- Thesis-first slide titles. Headlines 34–54pt, key figures 30–60pt, body generally 17–23pt, footnotes ≥ 9pt. Font: Inter (with safe fallback). Check Cyrillic and PDF export. Correct wrapping is more important than pushing font size down.
- Visual variety with coherence: dark cover / closing, white evidence pages, market bands, competitive matrix, chart, timeline, operating flow. One message per slide.
- Use a single strong accent with proper text contrast. Accent text on dark backgrounds must stay readable. Limit borders, shadows, gradients and decorative icons.

## Data graphics
- PAM/TAM/SAM/SOM: both audience and yearly revenue, same monetization scenario, clearly distinguish projected SOM from total potential. Label provenance and unit.
- EBITDA vs operating cash vs cumulative FCF vs investor distributions are **different series**. Label period, currency, scenario and hypothesis status; use the model, not copy-pasted figures.
- Growth scenarios require coherent scales; don't hide negative values with a truncated y-axis. Sources/qualifications in notes or appendix.

## Financial KPI priority
- Make **LTV/CAC** the visual hero when unit economics apply; pair with CAC, contribution LTV, Gross/Contribution margins and retention. Use verified cohort data or label projections as assumptions. No fabricated ratios.
- If LTV is inapplicable, omit the ratio and show applicable margin and cost metrics instead. Do not display N/A as success.

## Native editability
- Text boxes, shapes, tables and charts must be PowerPoint objects, not screenshot backgrounds. Crop only real product screenshots or photos.
- Render to PNG/PDF and review a contact sheet before final. Inspect full-resolution slides for wrapping and under-sized captions.
